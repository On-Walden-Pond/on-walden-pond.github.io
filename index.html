The Venue

A private setting, an hour from Atlanta

On Walden Pond is a family-run wedding and event venue on the quiet edge of Calhoun, Georgia — pines, still water, and a lighted bridge over the pond, kept simple so your day can be the focus.

Family owned and operated, we host events for groups both small and large — from baby showers and retirement parties to birthdays, engagements, and weddings. We take the time to know the families we work with, because a celebration should feel like it belongs to you.
