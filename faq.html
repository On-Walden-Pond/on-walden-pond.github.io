Good Questions

Answered before you ask.

The questions couples ask us most, answered plainly. Anything else is on the house — we answer everything personally.

How many guests can the property hold?

Up to 150 guests — and the whole property is yours alone. One celebration a day, never shared with another party.

What's included with a wedding?

The entire property: grill, fire pit, bridge, water features, covered pavilion and real bathrooms with hot water. The house to get ready in and the golf cart are available too — just ask.

What happens if it rains?

The covered pavilion seats your whole party — bar and fireplace included — so ceremonies and receptions run rain or shine. We'll walk you through your wet-weather plan together before the day.

Can we get married or shoot portraits without a full wedding?

Yes — elopements and portrait sessions by the hour. Early light on the fountain and the bridge is hard to beat.

How do we see the property in person?

Tours are by appointment — press Book a Tour and suggest a day that works for you; a family member will set the time.

Where exactly are you?

565 Owens Gin Road NE, Calhoun, Georgia — 29 private acres about an hour from Atlanta, far enough that it feels like another world.

Any extra-cost options on the day?

Fishing on the pond is available at extra cost — just tell us when you book. We're adding more options over time.

How fast will we hear back?

Within two business days — from a real family member, not an event company. Same phone, same family, every time.

Something else on your mind? Just ask — we answer everything personally.
