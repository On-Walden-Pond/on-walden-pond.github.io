Choose a Direction

Ten ways this website could look

Every design below uses the family's real photos. Open them, wander around, and tell us which one feels like the place — we'll build the final site from that one. You can also mix (for example: “the photos of #2 with the softness of #4”).

✓ Decided (Oct-12): the family picked #09 — Slide. The main site at on-walden-pond.github.io now wears that design; this page stays as the design archive.

Each design has now been updated with the things venue guests (and Google) look for everywhere: a Schedule a Tour button beside “check your date”, the one celebration per day promise said out loud, plain facts up front (location, distance, policy), and a note about elopements & photo sessions by the hour.
