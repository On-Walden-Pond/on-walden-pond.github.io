Answered before you ask.

The questions that have been asked most, answered plainly. Anything else? Just ask — we answer everything personally.

How many guests can the property hold?

Up to 150 guests — and the whole property is yours alone. One celebration a day, never shared with another party.

What's included with a wedding?

The entire property. Tables and chairs for your guest count come with the day — and so do the wooden bar, stage with electricity, stone fireplace, fridge and freezer. The golf cart is available with one of our drivers, and so is the house to get ready in — for weddings, just ask.

How many ceremony sites do you have?

Four, all on the property: fountain-side (the fountain can be turned off on request), dockside on the bridge, the pavilion lawn, and the back deck of the house — plus smaller nooks for two-person vows. Other spots are open with venue approval; there's a good backdrop every fifty feet.

What happens if it rains?

The covered pavilion seats your whole party — bar and fireplace included — and it's the wet-weather home for any ceremony site. Every outdoor location is also tent-friendly, and small weddings can move inside the house. We'll walk you through the rain plan together before you book.

Can we stay the night?

Yes — the house has a large bedroom with its own bath. The house is part of weddings only, and overnight use covers the day before and the day of your wedding — an extra cost, not a whole-weekend rental. Just ask when you set your date.

Do we need to rehearse?

It's worth it — a rehearsal keeps the ceremony flowing. We can open the property for you within 24–48 hours of your event; if you're on the day-before-and-day-of package, the rehearsal day is already built in.

Are pets allowed?

The grounds and the house stay pet-free, with service animals always welcome. If you have a situation, tell us — we'll find the best way to accommodate you.

Sparklers? Confetti?

Sparklers — yes, and they make a beautiful send-off. For the send-off, stick to leave-no-trace items — real petals, bubbles, sparklers. No glitter, rice, confetti or artificial petals.

Can we serve alcohol?

Yes — just make sure whoever serves it, caterer or bartender, holds a liquor license. That's the only rule.

Any curfew?

Music off by 10 p.m. — that's county law. The property is yours for your package hours.

Can we get married or shoot portraits without a full wedding?

Yes — elopements and portrait sessions by the hour, Monday through Thursday. Early light on the fountain and the bridge is hard to beat.

How do we see the property in person?

Tours are by appointment — press Book a Tour and suggest a day that works for you; a family member will set the time.

How do we book and hold our date?

Tell us your date, sign a short contract, and put down a 50% deposit — that holds the day. We take cash, check, Zelle and Venmo. The deposit isn't refundable, but with at least 30 days' notice we'll move you to another open date. There's no set lead time — if your date is open, you can book it.

Where exactly are you?

565 Owens Gin Road NE, Calhoun, Georgia — 30 private gated acres built around a stocked, spring-fed lake, about an hour from Atlanta and far enough that it feels like another world.

Are you the same property as The Falls at Whitetail Ranch?

Yes — the same 30 gated acres, the same falls, the same lake and pavilion. The property is under new ownership: the Little family, and this is our current site. thefallsatwhitetailranch.com is the old site. If you booked with us before or found us there, this is where to reach us now — with a new phone number and a new email.

Any extra-cost options on the day?

Fishing on the pond is available at extra cost — catch and release, bring your own gear. So is extra time on the property past your package hours, and an overnight stay in the house. Tell us what you have in mind when you book, and we'll price it for your day.

How fast will we hear back?

Within 24–48 hours — from a real family member, not an event company. Same phone, same family, every time.

Something else on your mind? Just ask — we answer everything personally.
