The Venue

A private setting, an hour from Atlanta

On Walden Pond is a family-run wedding venue on the quiet edge of Calhoun, Georgia — pines, open meadow, and still water, kept simple so your day can be the focus.

We host intimate ceremonies and full receptions alike, and we take the time to know the families we work with. Everything here is shaped by one idea: a celebration should feel like it belongs to you.
